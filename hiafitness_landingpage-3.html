Frequently asked questions

I’m a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. We start with your current level, learn the basics properly and build confidence step by step.

Can I combine personal training and online coaching?

Yes. Many clients use in-person sessions for technique and accountability, while following their individual plan independently through the app.

What if I can only train once or twice a week?

That is completely fine. Consistency matters more than perfection — we build a realistic plan around the time you actually have.

How do I know which package is right for me?

Book a free consultation. We will look at your goals, schedule and preferences, and I will recommend the option that fits best.
